QUEENS GAZETTE SUPPORT

How to play

Place exactly one queen in every row, every column and every colored region. Queens may not touch diagonally.

Help and Mistakes

Help can indicate cells ruled out by queens already on the board. The Mistakes counter records a queen placement when that placement leaves the current puzzle with no valid solution.

Daily Puzzle and statistics

Complete the Daily Puzzle to build your streak. Gameplay statistics and settings are stored locally and can be reset in the app.

Advertising and privacy

Queens Gazette uses banner advertising. Where applicable, advertising privacy choices can be opened from Settings.

Reporting a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the issue when contacting support.
