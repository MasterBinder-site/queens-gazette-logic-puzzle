PRIVACY POLICY

Queens Gazette · Updated October 3, 2026

Overview

Queens Gazette does not require an account. Gameplay progress, settings and statistics are stored locally on your device.

Advertising and data collection

Queens Gazette uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process coarse location derived from IP address, device identifiers, product interaction data, advertising data, crash data and performance data. Depending on Google's services and the user's consent choices, data may be used for advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and service improvement.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's consent management platform. Available advertising privacy choices can be accessed from Settings.

Local game data

Settings, games played, completed puzzles, best times and Daily Puzzle streaks are stored locally on the device.

Third-party services

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and the user's consent choices.

Contact

For help or privacy questions, use the contact information on the App Store listing or visit the support page.
